# 5. Şarkı: Mavi Vatan (deniz ve su altı takımları)

- **Eser adı:** Mavi Vatan
- **Süre hedefi:** 3:00
- **Radyodaki yeri:** "Karada ve denizde" arasından sonra

## Style of Music

```
Turkish pop rock anthem, 110 BPM, starts with sea waves and harbor ambience then builds, bright electric guitars, warm bass, big drums in chorus, uplifting male and female duet vocals, stadium choir on final chorus, clean modern production, hopeful and proud
```

## Exclude Styles

```
spoken directives, meta text, narration of instructions, sad, lo-fi, trap, screaming, military march
```
